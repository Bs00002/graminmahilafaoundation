# Gramin Mahila Foundation (Official Website)

Official website for **Gramin Mahila Foundation**, dedicated to rural women's empowerment, vocational skills training (sewing, fabric painting), student education support, tree plantation, and disaster relief in Sanand, Ahmedabad District, Gujarat, India.

---

## 🌸 Core Programs & Initiatives

1. **Women Vocational Training & Skill Center**: Hands-on tailoring, motor sewing machine training, fabric painting, and handicraft development.
2. **Girls' Self-Defence Camps**: Safety and confidence-building workshops for village girls and young women.
3. **Student Education Support**: Distribution of school stationery, notebooks, bags, and educational kits to rural children.
4. **Environment & Tree Plantation**: Planting native trees and fruit saplings across village schools and public grounds with long-term care.
5. **Disaster & Flood Relief**: Providing direct emergency assistance, ration supplies, and drinking water during seasonal monsoons and crises.
