Memorable moments from our community celebrations and field camps.

National Celebration

Republic Day Flag Hoisting & Community Assembly

Village elders, women trainees, and young school students gathered together for national celebrations. The event featured inspirational speeches on women's education, cultural songs by students, and sweet distribution.

  • Participation by over 100 village residents and youth
  • Felicitation of outstanding students and women trainees
  • Inspirational messages on village unity and self-reliance

Safety Workshop

Sanand Girls Self-Defence Camp

A landmark community camp focused on equipping village schoolgirls with practical self-defence moves, mental alertness, and fearlessness. The training was enthusiastically received by participating students and parents.

  • Demonstration of martial arts techniques by certified trainers
  • Interactive sessions on handling emergency situations safely
  • Appreciation certificates distributed to all participating girls
