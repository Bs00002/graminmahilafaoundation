Your Impact

How Your Support Reaches the Field

Every rupee donated or contributed in kind is used responsibly with strict ground accountability.

🧵

Sponsoring Sewing Equipment

Providing sewing machines and stitching toolkits to women who complete their vocational course, empowering them to start earning immediately.

🎒

Child Education Kits

Funding school bags, notebooks, and learning supplies for children from rural households who cannot afford basic school materials.

🍲

Emergency Food & Ration

Supplying wholesome grains, pulses, and cooking oil to elderly villagers, widows, and families stranded during monsoon flooding.

🌳

Sapling Plantation

Supporting the purchase, planting, and tree guards for native saplings to improve environmental health across village commons.

Our Promise

Our Commitment to Donor Transparency

At Gramin Mahila Foundation, we honor the trust placed in us by every donor and supporter. We maintain careful documentation of all activities and invite our supporters to visit our center in Sanand to observe our training classes in person.

  • Regular photo updates and direct reporting from program centers
  • Respect for donor preferences, privacy, and acknowledgement wishes
  • Clear separation between official foundation contacts and donor information
  • Zero unverified financial claims or speculative statements
