About Grameen Mahila Foundation

Rooted in rural Gujarat, working with dedication to empower women, nurture children, and bring care to families who need support.

Who We Are

Dedicated to Real Grassroots Change

Grameen Mahila Foundation is an authentic non-profit social organization established to create sustainable opportunities for women, children, and underprivileged families living in rural areas.

We believe that real change begins in the village home. When a rural woman learns how to operate a sewing machine, design fabrics, or manage family health, the whole community moves forward. Our initiatives are practical, respectful, and focused on helping people help themselves.

🎯 Our Vision

Every rural woman lives with dignity, self-confidence, and independent financial security.

❤️ Our Mission

To provide accessible skills training, student education support, environmental care, and compassionate community relief across Gujarat villages.

What Guides Us

Our Guiding Values

Every class we teach, every tree we plant, and every relief kit we deliver is driven by four core principles.

🤝

Dignity & Respect

We serve every person with deep respect. We do not treat anyone as a charity case, but as a valued partner in village progress.

🌱

Practical Skills

We teach skills that translate directly into livelihood opportunities—such as tailoring, fabric art, and craft creation.

🛡️

Honesty & Transparency

We share real photographs, verified contact information, and authentic ground-level reports without exaggeration.

🏡

Community First

We work directly in villages and neighborhoods with local participation, ensuring help reaches those who need it most.

Leena ben Kaushalkumar Patel Guiding Presence & Community Stewardship
Guiding Presence

Leena ben Kaushalkumar Patel

Dedicated Commitment to Rural Women's Self-Reliance & Welfare

At Grameen Mahila Foundation, leadership is defined by direct ground presence and personal dedication. Leena ben plays a central, active role in organizing, running, and supporting the foundation's day-to-day initiatives across Sanand and rural Gujarat.

Her commitment is reflected in her continuous presence at our vocational training centers, where she regularly meets with learners, listens to their family challenges, and ensures every woman has access to quality sewing machines, tailoring tools, and art materials. Her sincere encouragement has given hundreds of village mothers and daughters the confidence to learn, earn, and build a brighter future for their households.

Our Main Training Hub

Sanand Women's Training Center

Our training hub in Sanand, Ahmedabad District, serves as an active learning center for women from surrounding villages. Here, experienced instructors conduct hands-on classes in machine sewing, garment cutting, and fabric painting in a safe, friendly, and encouraging atmosphere.

  • Equipped with working sewing machines for daily practice
  • Experienced female instructors providing step-by-step guidance
  • Free learning kits and materials for enrolled trainees
  • Regular workshops on health, savings, and women's rights
