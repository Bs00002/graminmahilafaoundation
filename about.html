🌾 Rooted in Rural Gujarat

Dedicated to Real Grassroots Change & Women's Self-Reliance

Gramin Mahila Foundation is an authentic non-profit social organization established to create sustainable opportunities for women, children, and underprivileged families living in rural areas.

We believe that real change begins in the village home. When a rural woman learns how to operate a sewing machine, design fabrics, or manage family health, the whole community moves forward. Our initiatives are practical, respectful, and focused on helping people help themselves.

🎯

Our Vision

Every rural woman lives with dignity, self-confidence, and independent financial security through practical vocational skills.

❤️

Our Mission

To provide accessible skills training, student education support, environmental care, and compassionate community relief across Gujarat villages.

Guiding Presence

Leena ben Kaushalkumar Patel

At Gramin Mahila Foundation, leadership is defined by direct ground presence and personal dedication. Leena ben plays a central, active role in organizing, running, and supporting the foundation's day-to-day initiatives across Sanand and rural Gujarat. Through continuous on-site encouragement at our training centers, she listens to learners' family challenges and ensures women have access to quality sewing machines, tools, and educational kits.

"Real empowerment begins when a woman acquires a practical skill in her hands, enabling her to stand tall with dignity and care for her family."

Our Main Training Hub

Sanand – Nidhrad Women's Training Center

Our training hub located at Village Nidhrad on Sanand - Kadi Road (Taluka Sanand, Ahmedabad District) serves as an active learning center for women from surrounding rural communities. Here, experienced instructors conduct hands-on classes in machine sewing, garment cutting, and fabric painting in a safe, friendly, and encouraging atmosphere.

  • Equipped with working sewing machines for daily practice
  • Experienced female instructors providing step-by-step guidance
  • Free learning kits and materials for enrolled trainees
  • Regular workshops on health, savings, and women's rights
