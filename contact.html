Visiting Our Nidhrad Training Center

We believe in open doors and genuine grassroots hospitality. Here is what to know before visiting our training center:

How to Reach Us

Located on the Sanand - Kadi Road in Village Nidhrad (near Juni Panchayat Office, Tekra Vado Vas). Approx. 25 km west of Ahmedabad city center, accessible by bus and private transport.

🧵

See Active Training

During morning and afternoon hours, visitors can observe rural women practicing sewing, tailoring, machine maintenance, and art crafts.
