News & Field Updates

Read genuine field reports, milestone stories, and recent community updates from Grameen Mahila Foundation.

Recent Milestone · Sanand

New Batch of Village Women Completes Sewing Training

A dedicated cohort of women from surrounding villages successfully concluded their 3-month tailoring course at our Sanand training center. Trainees received personalized guidance on garment cutting, machine operation, and stitching finish.

Field Operation · Rural Aid

Emergency Ration Kits Delivered to Waterlogged Rural Households

During heavy seasonal waterlogging, our foundation relief volunteers mobilized essential dry food rations and clean drinking water to assist vulnerable families cut off from basic supply routes.

Environmental Drive · Village Greening

Community Plantation Drive Plants Over 200 Native Saplings

Local volunteers and village youth joined hands in planting hardy native trees along community spaces and school grounds, establishing a regular watering schedule to ensure high sapling survival rates.

Youth Workshop · Sanand

Schoolgirls Learn Practical Safety Techniques at Self-Defence Camp

Over 50 young girls participated in a multi-day self-defence workshop focused on practical release maneuvers, physical fitness, and mental confidence to travel safely to school and higher classes.

Art & Craft · Creative Livelihood

Fabric Painting Workshop Explores Traditional Textile Art

Women trainees created vibrant textile pieces using non-toxic fabric colors and traditional Indian patterns, opening new avenues for supplemental household income through handcrafted goods.

Community Gathering · Celebration

Flag Hoisting Ceremony Celebrated with Village Families

Community members, youth, and foundation staff gathered together in unity to celebrate national pride and reaffirm our commitment to empowering rural women and nurturing children.
