Volunteer With Us

Join hands to make a real difference in the lives of rural women and children.

Be The Change

Why Volunteer With Gramin Mahila Foundation?

Volunteering with us means rolling up your sleeves and working directly in rural communities. You will see firsthand how a small amount of your time can inspire a village girl to continue learning or help a mother earn her own living.

🤝

Real Grassroots Impact

Work directly with women, children, and village families. Every hour you spend creates immediate, visible results on the ground.

🌱

Hands-on Field Experience

From organizing tree drives to assisting in vocational workshops, you will gain practical leadership and community development skills.

📜

Volunteer Appreciation

We provide a formal Certificate of Volunteering and recommendation letters recognizing your dedicated hours and community service.

Open Roles

How You Can Contribute Your Time

Women's Workshop

Skill Training Assistant

Help our vocational trainers teach sewing, pattern cutting, embroidery, or handicraft skills to rural women at our Sanand center.

  • Time Commitment: 3-4 hours per week (flexible)
  • Location: Sanand Skill Center
  • Ideal for: Tailoring enthusiasts, fashion students, craft makers
Child Education

Student Tutor & Mentor

Provide homework assistance, basic math, reading support, and English spoken practice to rural school students after class hours.

  • Time Commitment: 2-3 hours on weekends or weekdays
  • Location: Village Community Centers around Sanand
  • Ideal for: College students, teachers, working professionals
Environment Drive

Tree Plantation Drive Helper

Join our weekend tree planting drives across villages, plant native saplings, install protective guards, and spread environmental awareness.

  • Time Commitment: Sunday mornings (4 hours)
  • Location: Rural Sanand & Ahmedabad outskirts
  • Ideal for: Nature lovers, youth groups, corporate volunteers
Creative Arts

Art & Painting Workshop Facilitator

Encourage creativity among rural children and women by conducting engaging drawing, painting, and craft-making sessions.

  • Time Commitment: Saturday / Sunday workshops
  • Location: Sanand Workshop Center
  • Ideal for: Artists, art students, hobby painters
Field Relief

Community Relief & Event Helper

Assist in packing food kits, organizing beneficiary lines, distributing supplies, and managing crowd coordination during community relief camps.

  • Time Commitment: On-call during relief drives & events
  • Location: Field distribution spots in Gujarat
  • Ideal for: Energetic team players and local youth
Documentation

Photography & Field Documentation

Capture genuine photographs, record short video stories of our beneficiaries, and help share real grassroots stories on our website and social updates.

  • Time Commitment: 4-6 hours per event
  • Location: Sanand center and project villages
  • Ideal for: Photographers, content creators, media students

Volunteer Guidelines

We believe in respectful, humble, and practical service. Here is what we expect from all volunteers:

✓
Respect Community Culture

Always communicate politely with village elders, women, and children.

✓
Punctuality & Commitment

If you commit to a batch or event, please arrive on time so activities run smoothly.

✓
Dignity & Privacy

Treat all beneficiaries as partners in progress, not as subjects of sympathy.
