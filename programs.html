Our Foundation Programs

Explore our structured community programs designed to build practical skills, support child learning, and protect the environment.

3-Month Course

1. Sewing & Tailoring Vocational Training

A structured practical course designed for village women and adolescent girls with zero prior tailoring experience. Conducted at our Sanand center with dedicated sewing machines and female instructors.

  • Module 1: Machine basics, pedal control, needle replacement, and maintenance.
  • Module 2: Body measurement, paper pattern drafting, and clean cloth cutting.
  • Module 3: Stitching blouses, kurtis, salwar suits, and children’s clothing.
  • Module 4: Pricing, customer management, and independent home enterprise guidance.

Hands-on Workshop

2. Fabric Painting & Handicrafts Studio

An artistic skill-building program helping rural women create handmade textile products that can be marketed locally or through craft exhibitions.

  • Techniques: Freehand brush painting, stencil art, block print motifs, and tie-dye.
  • Products: Dupattas, cushion covers, tote bags, table runners, and wall hangings.
  • Materials: Quality non-toxic fabric paints and materials provided free of cost.
  • Outcome: Turning creative talent into supplementary household earnings.

Year-Round Program

3. Student Learning & Education Support

We provide continuous academic assistance and educational materials to students from low-income rural households, ensuring poverty never stops a child from learning.

  • School Supplies: Notebooks, pens, geometry sets, and school bags distributed annually.
  • Fee Sponsorship: Financial support for school tuition fees for deserving students.
  • Girls’ Education: Active engagement with village parents to stop early dropouts.
  • Remedial Tutoring: Evening study sessions for children needing extra help in basic subjects.

Weekend & Vacation Camps

4. Self-Defence & Safety Camps for Girls

Focused workshops held in rural schools and community centers to build courage, awareness, and self-defence skills for schoolgirls and young women.

  • Physical Moves: Basic release techniques, blocking, and escape maneuvers.
  • Mental Readiness: Situational alertness, posture, and confident verbal response.
  • Safety Knowledge: Emergency helplines, peer support, and cyber safety guidance.
  • Impact: Overcoming fear and encouraging girls to travel safely to schools and colleges.

Community Environment

5. Green Villages Tree Plantation Initiative

Our tree planting initiative connects community members with environmental stewardship, creating cooler, greener villages.

  • Native Saplings: Planting hardy trees that thrive in Gujarat climate (Neem, Peepal, Jamun).
  • Public Spaces: Planting along rural school compounds, village ponds, and common roads.
  • Tree Adoption: Local youth take responsibility for regular watering and protection.
  • Awareness: Teaching children about soil health, air quality, and nature conservation.
Volunteer for Plantation
Community Empowerment

6. Women's Awareness & Rights Drives

Community gatherings and interactive workshops educating rural women on health, nutrition, legal rights, and financial literacy.

  • Health & Hygiene: Practical sessions on maternal care, menstrual hygiene, and village sanitation.
  • Financial Literacy: Assistance with zero-balance bank accounts, postal savings, and self-help groups.
  • Government Schemes: Informing families about welfare pensions, widow support, and child education schemes.
  • Community Dialogue: Creating a safe platform for women to share challenges and find collective solutions.

Nutritional Aid

7. Food & Nutrition Relief Drives

Direct meal and grocery assistance ensuring that vulnerable rural families, elderly individuals, and malnourished children receive wholesome sustenance.

  • Prepared Meals: Fresh, hygienic, and warm meals distributed during community drives.
  • Dry Ration Kits: Bags containing wheat flour, rice, lentils, cooking oil, and essential spices.
  • Elderly & Child Care: Prioritizing senior citizens living alone and young children in distress.
  • Direct Impact: 100% genuine ground distribution with complete transparency.
